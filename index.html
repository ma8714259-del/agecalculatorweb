The Art & Science of Age Calculation

How Exact Age Is Calculated

Calculating age isn't simple subtraction. It requires accounting for varying month lengths (28–31 days), leap years every four years, and precise time-zone alignment. Our calculator uses millisecond-precision arithmetic for flawless results.

Why Leap Years Matter

Earth orbits the Sun in 365.2422 days. Every four years we add February 29th. Anyone born on a leap day has a unique age trajectory that only a precise calculator can capture.

Age in Official Contexts

From passport applications to school enrollment, pension eligibility and medical milestones — an exact, verifiable age is essential. Our tool delivers results accurate to the second.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the age calculator work?

It computes the exact milliseconds between your birth date and today's date, then converts them into years, months, days, hours, minutes and seconds using real calendar rules.

Do I need to enter today's date?

No! Just enter your date of birth. Our calculator automatically uses today's date to compute your age instantly.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. All calculations happen in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is sent to any server.

Why are months, weeks and days counted differently?

Each unit is a separate accumulation. Months count calendar months precisely, and weeks and days are counted from your birth with leap years included.
