The Art & Science of Age Calculation

How Exact Age Is Calculated

Calculating age isn't simple subtraction. It requires accounting for varying month lengths (28–31 days), leap years every four years, and precise time-zone alignment. Our calculator uses millisecond-precision arithmetic to give you a flawless result.

Why Leap Years Matter

Earth orbits the Sun in 365.2422 days. Every four years, we add February 29th to keep our calendar aligned. Anyone born on a leap day ("leapling") has a unique age trajectory that only a precise calculator can capture.

Age in Official Contexts

From passport applications to school enrollment cutoffs, pension eligibility, and medical milestones — an exact, verifiable age is essential. Our tool delivers results accurate to the second.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the age calculator work?

It computes the exact milliseconds between your birth date and the target date, then converts them into years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds using real calendar rules.

Can I calculate my age on a past or future date?

Yes! Use the optional "Calculate Age On" field to select any date, past or future.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. All calculations happen in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is sent to any server.

Why are the numbers in months, weeks and days different?

Each unit is a separate accumulation. "Months" is not simply years × 12; it counts calendar months precisely. Similarly, weeks and days are counted from your birth with leap years included.
