How Age Is Calculated

Calculating age requires accounting for varying month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) and leap years. Our age calculator determines the exact duration elapsed between your date of birth and today's date or any custom date you select.

Why Exact Age Matters for Official Documents

For passport applications, school admissions, visa processing, and legal contracts, precise age calculation is vital. Using an automated tool ensures accurate reporting down to the day.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my age manually?

Subtract your birth year from the current year, adjusting if your birthday has not occurred yet this year.

Does this calculator account for leap years?

Yes, leap years are fully accounted for when determining total days, hours, and seconds.
